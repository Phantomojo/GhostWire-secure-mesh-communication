## 📊 Instagram Specifications 2026

### Feed Posts
- **Size:** 1080×1080px (1:1) or 1080×1350px (4:5 portrait)
- **Format:** MP4 (H.264) or PNG
- **Max Duration:** 60 seconds
- **Caption:** 2,200 characters (optimal: 150-200)
- **Hashtags:** 3-10 recommended (Instagram now limits hashtag reach)

### Stories
- **Size:** 1080×1920px (9:16 vertical)
- **Format:** MP4 or PNG
- **Max Duration:** 60 seconds per story
- **Features:** Polls, questions, links (with 10k+ followers)

### Reels
- **Size:** 1080×1920px (9:16 vertical)
- **Format:** MP4 (H.264, AAC audio)
- **Max Duration:** 90 seconds
- **Frame Rate:** 30fps recommended
- **Audio:** Trending audio boosts discovery

### Carousels
- **Slides:** Up to 10 images/videos
- **Size:** 1080×1080px (1:1)
- **Format:** PNG or MP4
- **Engagement:** 0.55% average (higher than Reels at 0.50%)

⚙️ How Does Onion Routing Work?

Like layers of an onion, your message gets wrapped in encryption:

1. Your message
2. + Layer 1 encryption (for node A)
3. + Layer 2 encryption (for node B)
4. + Layer 3 encryption (for node C)

Each node only sees:
- Who sent it to them
- Who to send it to next

No single node knows:
- The original sender
- The final destination
- The message content

This is Sphinx routing. This is GhostWire.

## 📞 Crisis Management

### If Launch Has Issues
1. **Acknowledge** - Post story about the issue
2. **Update** - Regular progress updates
3. **Resolve** - Fix and announce resolution
4. **Learn** - Post-mortem and improvements

### If Negative Feedback
1. **Listen** - Understand the concern
2. **Respond** - Thoughtful, not defensive
3. **Act** - Take action if valid
4. **Follow Up** - Show what changed
