## 🎯 Who Needs a Commercial License?

You need a commercial license if you want to:

### **❌ Not AGPL-Compliant Use Cases**

- **Proprietary Products**: Integrate GhostWire into closed-source software
- **SaaS Without Source Disclosure**: Offer GhostWire as a service without releasing modifications
- **Closed-Source Derivatives**: Create modified versions without sharing source code
- **Commercial Hosting**: Host GhostWire for customers without AGPL compliance
- **OEM Integration**: Embed GhostWire in commercial products
- **Trademark Use**: Use GhostWire name/logo in commercial offerings
- **Custom Terms**: Need licensing terms different from AGPL-3.0

### **✅ AGPL-Compliant Use Cases (No Commercial License Needed)**

- Personal use and experimentation
- Research and educational projects
- Internal company tools (with source available to employees)
- Open source projects (released under AGPL-3.0)
- Community deployments (with full source disclosure)
