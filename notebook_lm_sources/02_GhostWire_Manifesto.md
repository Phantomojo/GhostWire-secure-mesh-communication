# GhostWire Manifesto: Origins & Ethics

> "We do not build to disrupt. We build to survive."

## 1. The Origin: Maandamano 2024/2025
GhostWire was not born in a boardroom; it was conceived on the streets.
During the protests (Maandamano) in Kenya, a singularity occurred: **The Digital Blackout.**
For the first time, a generation realized that their "freedom of speech" was entirely dependent on corporate and government infrastructure. The "Off Switch" is real.

GhostWire is the response to that fear.
*   **The Joke that became a Tool:** Started as a satirical "what if" project, it evolved as the reality of digital authoritarianism became undeniable globally (Bangladesh, Gaza, Ukraine).

---

## 2. The Core Tenets

### I. Connection is a Human Right
It should not be metered, monitored, or revoked. GhostWire utilizes the *physics* of radio waves (Bluetooth, WiFi) which cannot be legislated away easily.

### II. Infrastructure Independence
We reject the reliance on "The Tower".
*   In **Gaza**, fiber lines are cut.
*   In **Ukraine**, power stations are bombed.
*   In **Kenya**, bandwidth is throttled.
GhostWire treats **Every Device as Infrastructure**. If you have a battery and a wifi chip, you are the ISP.

### III. The Knowledge Ark (Civilization Backup)
Survival is more than just secure chat. It is about accessing information.
GhostWire nodes serve as "Lifeboats" for human knowledge. A node in a rural village doesn't just pass messages; it hosts:
*   Medical Textbooks (Where There Is No Doctor)
*   Educational Resources (Khan Academy, Wikipedia)
*   Agricultural Guides
This moves the value proposition from "Privacy" (abstract) to "Utility" (concrete).

---

## 3. The "Cyberpunk" Reality
The user noted: *"The internet is looking more like Cyberpunk 2077."*
This is the core aesthetic and philosophical driver of GhostWire.
*   **The Corporate Net:** Walled gardens, AI-generated noise, surveillance capitalism.
*   **The GhostWire:** The "Dark Web" in the positive sense—the hidden, human, messy, free web that exists in the shadows of the giants.

## 4. Business Model & Ethics
We solve the tension between "Doing Good" and "Surviving".
*   **No Data Harvesting:** We physically *cannot* read user messages due to E2EE (End-to-End Encryption). We have nothing to sell to advertisers.
*   **Sustainable Revenue:** We sell **Resilience as a Service**.
    *   **NGOs/Journalists:** Pay for "Enterprise Deployment Tools" (e.g., mass-configuring 500 nodes for a mission).
    *   **Hardware:** Pre-flashed "GhostWire Sticks" (Raspberry Pi Zeros) sold at a margin.
    *   **The Community:** Gets the software for free, forever.

## 5. Summary
GhostWire is a **Civil Defense Project** disguised as a chat app. It is the digital equivalent of a water filter or a solar panel—a tool for self-reliance in an unpredictable century.
