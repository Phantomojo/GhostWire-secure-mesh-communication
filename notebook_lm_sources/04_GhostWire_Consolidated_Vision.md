# GhostWire 2026: The Vision

> "Civilization's Backup Plan"

This document consolidates the strategic vision for GhostWire, integrating the **AI-Native Mesh** and **Knowledge Ark** concepts into a unified roadmap.
Use this as the "North Star" for development and the Red Bull Basement pitch.

---

## 1. The Core Philosophy
GhostWire is not just a chat app. It is a **Resilience Platform**.
In a world of increasing instability (internet blackouts, censorship, natural disasters), GhostWire ensures that **human connection and knowledge** survive.

*   **Current State:** A secure, decentralized mesh messenger (Rust + React).
*   **Future State:** An autonomous, AI-managed survival network that creates order from chaos.

---

## 2. The Manifesto: Why We Build
> "The Internet is becoming Cyberpunk 2077. GhostWire is the Resistance."

**1. The Maandamano Doctrine (Anti-Censorship)**
During the 2024/2025 protests, we learned a hard truth: **The Internet is a switch the government can turn off.**
GhostWire is built on the principle that *connection is a human right*. By using peer-to-peer physics (Bluetooth, WiFi Direct, LoRa), we remove the "Off Switch."

**2. The "Gaza/Ukraine" Reality (Infrastructure Independence)**
In war, cell towers are the first targets.
GhostWire doesn't need towers. It turns *rubble into routing*. If one phone survives, it's a node. If a truck moves between towns, it's a data mule.

**3. Ethical Sustainability (No Data Harvesting)**
We reject the "Extraction Economy" of Web2.
*   **No Ads, No Tracking:** We don't sell users.
*   **Sustainable Revenue:** We sell *Resilience*. Premium enterprise features (for NGOs, Hospitals) fund the free open-source tool for the people.

## 3. Pillar I: The AI-Native Mesh ("The Brain")
*Goal: Turn a chaotic swarm of devices into an intelligent organism.*

### The Problem
Mesh networks are messy. Nodes join/leave, bandwidth fluctuates, and bad actors (Sybil attacks) try to jam the signal. Humans can't manage this manually.

### The Solution: Autonomous Network Intelligence
1.  **Distributed Immunity:**
    *   Every node runs a lightweight AI model (simulated for now, real later).
    *   It monitors traffic patterns to detect **Sybil Attacks** (fake nodes) and **Jamming** without a central server.
    *   *Pitch:* "The network has an immune system that identifies viruses and isolates them automatically."

2.  **Predictive Routing (The "Mule" Logic):**
    *   The AI predicts where nodes *will be*.
    *   Example: A disaster relief truck adds a node. The AI knows it moves between the "City" and "Refugee Camp" daily. It preemptively loads messages onto the truck to ferry them across the gap.

---

## 3. Pillar II: The Knowledge Ark ("The Soul")
*Goal: Make the mesh useful even when no one is talking.*

### The Problem
A communication network is useless if you have nothing to say or valid info to share. When the internet falls, Wikipedia, Maps, and Medical Guides disappear.

### The Solution: The Portable Library of Alexandria
GhostWire nodes can be "seeded" with compressed knowledge packs (`.zim` files).

*   **Tier 1 (4GB - Phone):** Medical urgency guides, Survival manuals, Local Maps.
*   **Tier 2 (128GB - Dedicated Pi):** Full English Wikipedia (w/ images), Khan Academy, Engineering Specs.

**User Experience:**
You connect to GhostWire WiFi. You open your browser. You can search "How to treat cholera" or "Water pump repair" *instantly*, served from your neighbor's node.

## 4. Assessment & Path Forward

### The "Prototype Strategy"
You are currently building a **"Vertical Slice"**.
*   It is okay that the AI is currently simulated (`threat_found: 0` -> `threat_found: 1`).
*   It is okay that the mesh transport is buggy.
**Why?** Because you are selling the *vision*. Investors and Judges (Red Bull) back the **Idea** and the **Capability of the Founder**.

### Tech Stack Validation
*   **Rust (Backend):** Perfect choice. High performance, memory safe, runs on low-power devices. Hard to learn, but pays off in reliability.
*   **React/Vite (Frontend):** fast, responsive, modern. Standard industry choice.
*   **Architecture:** The "Core <-> UI" separation is professional grade. It allows you to run the Core headless on a Raspberry Pi later.

### Verdict
GhostWire is **over-ambitious** for a solo dev, but that is exactly what wins competitions.
By simulating the "Future Features" (AI, Ark) while polishing the "Core Features" (Chat, Connecting), you bridge the gap between "Dream" and "Product".
