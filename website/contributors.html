Contributor Roles Ladder

We believe in clear paths to ownership and responsibility. The GhostWire roles ladder outlines how to grow within our ecosystem, from your first contribution to steering core project decisions.

Level 1

Contributor

Anyone who contributes to the project through pull requests, documentation updates, translations, code review, or testing. Automatically granted upon first merged PR.

Level 2

Regular

A contributor who regularly submits code, actively participates in discussions, and reviews others' pull requests. Nominated by Maintainers after sustained engagement.

Level 3

Maintainer

Responsible for a specific subsystem or component (e.g., crypto_machine, libp2p transport, or webui). Has merge permissions for relevant code paths.

Level 4

Core Member

Members of the steering committee who guide the overall architecture, governance, license enforcement, and release roadmap. Decided by consensus among Core and Maintainers.
