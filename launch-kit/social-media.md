## LinkedIn Post

I'm excited to share GhostWire — a decentralized, censorship-resistant mesh
communication platform I've been building in Rust.

GhostWire enables peer-to-peer encrypted communication without any central
servers. It's designed for communities that lack reliable internet access,
disaster responders who need communication when infrastructure fails, and
anyone who values privacy.

Key technical highlights:
• Written in Rust (Tokio + libp2p + Axum)
• 7 transport layers with automatic fallback
• AI-powered routing trained on real GuifiSants mesh data
• End-to-end encryption (AES-256-GCM + X25519)
• Runs on a Raspberry Pi

The project is AGPL-3.0 licensed and part of our GCD4F 2026 competition
submission representing the Open University of Kenya.

We're actively looking for contributors. If you work with Rust, networking,
AI/ML, or security — I'd love to connect.

GitHub: https://codeberg.org/PHANTOMOJO/GhostWire-secure-mesh-communication
